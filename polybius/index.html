SUMMARY

            

              According to arcade folklore, a mysterious upright cabinet appeared
              in a handful of Portland arcades in the early 1980s. Players
              reportedly experienced intense psychedelic visuals, headaches,
              amnesia, and night terrors. Men in black suits allegedly collected
              the machines weeks later — leaving no paperwork, no invoices,
              and no surviving boards.
            

            

              Historians of gaming culture treat Polybius as a
              creepypasta ancestor: a story that grew from
              half-remembered rumors, Tempest-like vector aesthetics, and Cold War
              anxiety about psychological testing. No manufacturer records,
              patents, or verified photographs have ever surfaced.
            

          

        

        

          
FOLKLORE NOTES
